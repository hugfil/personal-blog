Quick heads-up on where this blog is going next: I'm bringing AI into my day-to-day life as a tester, and I'll be taking notes along the way. No hype, no magic wands, just what actually works (and what faceplants). 🎢

Here's my shortlist of things to poke at 🔍:

- ✍️ **Test case generation:** can AI turn user stories into solid test ideas, or just very confident nonsense?
- 🧪 **Test data:** realistic, varied, and no real customers harmed in the process.
- 🛠️ **Automation with an AI copilot:** faster Playwright and Cypress scripts, as long as I still read what it wrote.
- 🩹 **Flaky tests and self-healing:** because chasing a locator that changed overnight is nobody's idea of fun.
- 🔎 **Failure and log analysis:** letting AI do the first pass of "why did this break?"
- 🕵️ **Exploratory testing sidekick:** a second brain for "what if?" scenarios I haven't thought of.
- 🧠 **Testing AI itself:** because someone has to ask the chatbot the awkward questions.

Ground rules: AI assists, I decide. Every experiment gets checked, and I'll share the wins, the flops and the "well, that was unexpected" moments. 😄

Got a favourite use case or a good horror story? I'd love to hear it, though replies may be slow. Consider it a message in a bottle. 🍾
